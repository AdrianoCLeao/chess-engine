Chess piece artwork
===================

Sets under this folder are copied from Lichess (lichess-org/lila):

  - companion/  — Lichess "companion" piece set
  - cburnett/   — CBurnett set (also used for the app icon knight)
  - merida/     — Merida set (available, not active in UI)
  - alpha/, companion sample files as needed

Upstream source:
  https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece

CBurnett artwork originates from Colin M. L. Burnett (Wikimedia Commons),
typically licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. Other Lichess sets follow their upstream licenses.
This project does not claim ownership of the piece artwork.
